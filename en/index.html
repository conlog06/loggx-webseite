How a project works

Four steps. You always know what I'm working on.

  2. 1

    Initial consultation

    We talk on the phone or via video call for about an hour. You tell me about your business and what the site should do. Afterwards you get a fixed-price quote.

    Concept and design

    I build your home page first. That way you see early on what it will look like, and we can still adjust colours, copy and layout.

FAQ

Question not listed? Just write to me.

How long until my website is online?

A business-card site is usually online after 14 days, a business website after 3–4 weeks. What usually takes longest is gathering copy and photos – I'll help you with that.

What do you need from me?

Your logo (if you have one), a few photos of your business and a rough description of your services. I take care of structure, copy and design – of course in agreement with you.

Can I change my site myself later?

Yes. After launch I show you how to update text, images and opening hours yourself. If you'd rather hand that off, I'll make changes billed by the hour.

Do I own the website afterwards?

Completely. You receive all files and logins. You are not tied to me or to any particular system.

What does the site cost me per month?

Nothing from me. Domain and hosting are registered in your name directly with the provider (usually just a few euros a month) – I'll help you set them up. You only pay for changes & support if you order them (from €70 per hour). There is no subscription.

Do you work with English-speaking clients?

Yes. We can talk in English and I can build your site in English, German or both. Quotes and invoices are available in English as well.

I already have a domain. Is that a problem?

Not at all. The domain stays with you – I simply connect it to the new site. If you don't have one yet, I'll register one in your name.
